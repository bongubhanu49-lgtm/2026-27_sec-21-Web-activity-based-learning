Places to inspire your next trip

From timeless monuments to peaceful waterways, India offers a journey for every traveler.

Taj Mahal

Visit this world-famous marble mausoleum in Agra, celebrated for its history and architecture.

Best for: history and architecture

Kerala

Enjoy tranquil backwaters, lush landscapes, and the welcoming culture of southern India.

Best for: nature and relaxation

Jaipur

Explore grand forts, colorful markets, and the rich heritage of Rajasthan's capital.

Best for: forts and local culture

Travel tips

A little preparation helps make a trip more enjoyable.

  • Plan your route and check local opening hours.
  • Book transport and accommodation ahead when possible.
  • Keep valuables secure and carry copies of important documents.
  • Respect local customs and leave natural places clean.
